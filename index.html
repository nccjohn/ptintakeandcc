Step 1 — Introduce yourself & verify the patient

Start the encounter professionally, then verify the patient before beginning the intake.

10 points
Start every encounter the same way. First introduce yourself as the medical assistant, then verify the patient’s first name, last name, and date of birth.

Step 2 — Interview the patient

Ask the questions needed to fully develop the chief complaint and SAMPLE history.

50 points
Patient InterviewText-style transcript

Step 3 — Document the intake

Use the patient interview on the left and select the correct chart entry for each item on the right.

40 points
Patient InterviewReview while charting

The interview stays visible while you choose the documentation that matches what the patient reported.

Assessment instructions: Select one documentation answer for every item. You may change any selection before submitting. No answer is scored until you select Submit Documentation & Continue. Once submitted, your answers are locked for this attempt.
Documentation assessment: Complete CC, OPQRST, and SAMPLE using only the information collected during the interview. For Signs/Symptoms, select associated symptoms and pertinent negatives without repeating the chief complaint. Complete all OPQRST elements using the patient’s reported pain or discomfort.

Type the generated chief complaint and SAMPLE summary exactly as they should appear in the chart.
